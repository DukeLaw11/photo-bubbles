Common questions

How do I add or change photos?
Tap the gear (⚙️) in the top corner of the game, then tap any bubble to take a new photo, choose one from your library, or remove it.

How do I rename the game or change colors?
Same place — the gear opens Settings, where you can edit the title, title color, header color, and the background pattern.

How do I win?
Merge two of the biggest bubbles and they pop! That's the win — confetti and all. You can keep playing afterward in free-play mode.

Are my photos private?
Completely. They never leave your device. See our Privacy Policy — the short version is: we collect nothing.

Restarting from scratch
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears everything: photos, title, scores, and settings.
