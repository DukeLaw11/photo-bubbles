BUBBLE FACES!

The drop-and-merge game your family builds itself.

Most games come finished. This one comes open.

Bubble Faces hands the game to its player. Every one of the ten bubbles is a blank canvas waiting for a photo — and the moment kids realize they get to decide what goes in the game, it stops being an app and becomes theirs.

  • They pick the cast. Mom in the little bubble, the dog in the big one, a silly face for the boss bubble — every choice is the player's own.
  • They name it. The title screen says whatever they want, in big storybook letters — "JACK!" beats any logo we could design.
  • They set the mood. Title colors, header colors, and swappable emoji backgrounds — smiles, Christmas, Halloween, fireworks, or pets. Redecorate for every season, every whim.
  • They crown the winner. Even the victory celebration shows a photo they chose.

Then the game part takes over: drop bubbles with one finger, merge matching pairs into bigger ones, and pop the biggest for a confetti win. Simple enough for small hands, satisfying enough that grown-ups "test" it longer than they planned.

Creativity without a catch

All that openness comes with a closed door where it matters: the photos your family puts into the game never leave the device. No accounts, no ads, no uploads, no tracking — nothing to set up and nothing to worry about. Remake the game a hundred times; it all stays yours.
