Privacy Policy

Bubble Faces

Bubble Faces does not collect any data. Ever. From anyone.

Your photos stay on your device

The photos you add to the game are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app's own storage. They are never uploaded, transmitted, analyzed, or shared. The app has no servers and makes no network requests with your content.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party services, SDKs that collect data, or data sharing
  • No crash reports containing your content

Children's privacy

Bubble Faces is designed to be safe for children. Because the app collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. There are no links out of the game, no purchases inside the game, and no way for any content to leave the device.

Permissions the app asks for

The app requests access to your camera and photo library for one purpose only: so you can choose or take the photos that become the game's bubbles. Those photos are saved on your device and nowhere else. You can decline these permissions and still play with the built-in colors.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything — photos, the game title, scores, and settings. Nothing exists anywhere else to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices ever change, this page will be updated before any change takes effect. The commitment above — no data collection — is the design of the product, not a temporary policy.
